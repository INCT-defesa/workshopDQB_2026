The Workshop

An international scientific event connecting molecular design, synthesis, experimental characterization, and biological validation for chemical and biological defense.

Scientific Purpose

Connecting computational and experimental science

The I Virtual International Workshop on Chemical and Biological Defense is an initiative of INCT-Defesa designed to promote integration among scientific areas involved in the development of strategies and technologies for chemical and biological defense.

The workshop will bring together researchers and specialists working with computational and experimental approaches, providing a space for scientific discussion, knowledge exchange, and the development of new collaborations.

The scientific program was structured to connect complementary stages of research and development, from molecular design and computational modeling to synthesis, experimental characterization, biological evaluation, and validation.

Event Format

Two days • Four scientific areas

Date: December 3–4, 2026

Time: 08:00–12:00 (Brasília time)

Format: Fully online

Registration: Free of charge

Scientific program: Main lectures, Flash Talks, and opportunities for scientific exchange.

Molecular Modeling & Computational Design

Computational approaches, molecular modeling, and simulation strategies applied to chemical and biological defense.

Synthetic Strategies

Development and synthesis of molecules and materials for chemical and biological defense applications.

Experimental Characterization

Experimental methods for the characterization of molecules, materials, and systems relevant to defense applications.

Biological Evaluation & Validation

Biological evaluation and experimental validation of molecules, materials, and defense technologies.
